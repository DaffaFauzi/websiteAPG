# Core Enterprise Products for Industries, with Key Modules / Features

Here are several product ideas along with what modules they should include and why they’re needed.

---

## **1. ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) Platform**

This is still very high demand, especially if you build vertical-specific, AI-enhanced, or modern versions.  

### **Key Modules / Features**
- Finance & Accounting (general ledger, AP/AR, cash flow, budgeting)  
- Procurement & Supplier Management  
- Inventory & Warehouse Management  
- Manufacturing / Production Planning & Scheduling (BOM, MRP, routing)  
- Human Resources & Payroll  
- Sales / Order to Cash (CRM, quotations, pricing, shipping)  
- Project Management & Costing  
- Quality Assurance, Maintenance, Asset Management (for industrial sectors)  
- Reporting, Analytics, Dashboards, KPI tracking  
- Regulatory / Compliance (local laws, ESG, safety, environmental)  

### **Trends & Opportunities**
- **Industry-specific or vertical ERP** (manufacturing, construction, healthcare, logistics) to reduce customization cost/time.

- **AI & ML-driven modules** (forecasting, anomaly detection, automation) integrated into ERP.  
  *Sources:* blog.forgestik.com (+2), Spinnaker Support (+2)

- **Cloud/hybrid deployment, microservices, modular/composable architecture.**  
  *Sources:* blog.forgestik.com (+3), Software Connect (+3), priority-software.com (+3)

- **IoT / edge integration** for real-time sensing & data from physical assets.  
  *Sources:* Deskera (+2), Software Connect (+2)

- **Mobile access, workflow automation, smarter UX.**  
  *Sources:* Software Connect (+2), Deskera (+2)

## **2. KPI / Performance Management / Business Intelligence Platform**

This can be a standalone product or an extension of ERP — especially important for enterprises to monitor, optimize, and benchmark performance.

### **Key Modules / Features**
- KPI definition & management (financial, operational, HR, supply chain, etc.)  
- Dashboards and scorecards (real-time, historical, predictive)  
- Benchmarking & comparisons (across branches, regions, global standard)  
- Automated alerting / escalation when KPIs deviate from targets  
- Scenario planning / forecasting (what-if, sensitivity analysis)  
- Integrations with ERP, CRM, SCM, HR systems, external data sources  

**Why it's needed:** Enterprises want actionable insights, not just raw data. Helps with management, decision making, compliance, and accountability.

---

## **3. Supply Chain & Logistics Management**

For industries where goods, raw materials, and distribution are important (manufacturing, retail, agriculture, FMCG, etc.).

### **Modules / Features**
- Demand forecasting & planning  
- Supplier & vendor management, sourcing optimization  
- Order and transport management, route planning, EDI, cross-dock  
- Warehouse & inventory optimization, tracking, multi-location  
- Cold chain monitoring, IoT-sensor integration, traceability  
- Returns & reverse logistics  

**Why it matters:** With increasing complexity, this is an area that often needs specialized vertical solutions.

---

## **4. Asset Management / Maintenance & Operations (MRO, Field Service)**

Valuable especially for infrastructure, manufacturing, utilities, transport, energy, and construction.

### **Key Features**
- Asset registry, lifecycle tracking, depreciation  
- Preventive & predictive maintenance planning (using IoT/data)  
- Work order management, scheduling, SLA tracking, technician dispatch  
- Spare parts inventory & procurement  
- Safety, compliance, inspections, audit trails

## **5. Compliance / Risk / Regulatory Management**

Enterprise demand is growing for tools to manage legal, safety, environmental, financial, and data/privacy regulations.

### **Features**
- Regulation tracking & updates database  
- Policy & procedure management, document control & auditing  
- Risk assessment, incident / issue reporting & tracking  
- Compliance dashboards, scorecards, audit trail  
- Automated workflows & alerts for non-compliance

### **Modules / Features**
- Recruitment / Onboarding / Applicant Tracking  
- Performance management, KPI for employees, goal alignment

## **7. Production / Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) + Quality + Traceability**

Especially for industrial sectors, heavily regulated products, and complex manufacturing.

### **Modules / Features**
- Shop floor control, work order scheduling, real-time production monitoring
- BOM & version control, routing, revisions, change management
- Quality & inspection management, test data, defect tracking
- Traceability (lot / serial / batch), compliance reporting
- Integration with ERP and IoT sensors

## **8. Sustainability & ESG Management Platform**

Critical for enterprises facing increasing regulatory requirements, investor pressure, and consumer demand for environmental responsibility. ESG platforms help organizations track, report, and improve their environmental, social, and governance performance.

### **Key Modules / Features**
- ESG goal setting and target management
- Carbon footprint tracking & emissions monitoring
- Energy consumption & resource usage analytics
- Supply chain sustainability scoring & monitoring
- ESG reporting & disclosure management (CSRD, SASB, TCFD)
- Stakeholder engagement & materiality assessment
- Regulatory compliance tracking (EU Taxonomy, SEC rules)
- ESG KPIs & dashboard analytics
- Risk assessment for climate & sustainability factors
- Green financing & ESG investment tracking

### **Why it's Trending (2024-2025):**
- **Regulatory Pressure**: EU CSRD, SEC climate rules, and global sustainability reporting standards
- **Investor Demand**: ESG factors now represent 30-50% of investment analysis
- **Consumer Expectations**: 70% of consumers consider sustainability when making purchases
- **Risk Management**: Climate change impacts business operations and supply chains

## **9. AI-Powered Cybersecurity & Threat Intelligence Platform**

Essential in today's threat landscape where cyber attacks are becoming more sophisticated with AI assistance. This platform provides proactive threat detection, automated response, and intelligence-driven security operations.

### **Key Modules / Features**
- AI-driven threat detection & anomaly analysis
- Automated incident response & remediation
- Threat intelligence gathering & analysis
- Behavioral analytics & user entity behavior analytics (UEBA)
- Zero-trust network access & identity management
- Security information & event management (SIEM)
- Endpoint detection & response (EDR/XDR)
- Vulnerability management & patch automation
- Compliance monitoring & audit trails
- Dark web monitoring & threat hunting
- AI-powered phishing detection & email security
- Cloud security posture management

### **Why it's Trending (2024-2025):**
- **Rising Cyber Threats**: Global cybercrime costs projected to reach $10.5T by 2025
- **AI-Powered Attacks**: Cybercriminals using AI tools for sophisticated attacks
- **Regulatory Requirements**: GDPR, CCPA, NIS2, and other data protection laws
- **Remote Work Security**: 70% increase in cyber attacks during remote work surge
- **Supply Chain Attacks**: SolarWinds, Kaseya, and MOVEit incidents highlight risks
